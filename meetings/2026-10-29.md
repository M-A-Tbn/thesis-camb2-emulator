## Evidence to show

Start with the [redshift comparison figure](../camb-comparison/01_current_solver_comparison/results/figures/01_solver_agreement_by_redshift.png).
Its horizontal axis is redshift. Each column is a cosmology; the top row shows
the largest absolute sampled disagreement and the bottom row shows RMS, which
summarizes the overall size of the differences without sign cancellation.
Lower values mean closer agreement with CLASS.

The largest sampled focus-interval disagreements across the nine redshifts are:

|---|---:|---:|
| Flat LambdaCDM | 0.1403% | 0.0692% |

All six maxima occur at z=2. The actual P(k) plots are supporting figures;
the nearly overlapping spectra do not display the small differences as clearly.

## Meaning and limits

**Observed:** CAMB2 has smaller maximum and RMS disagreement with CLASS in
all 27 cases in the stated interval. The pattern persists over the sampled
redshifts; CAMB2 need not be closer at every individual k.

**Assumptions:** the configurations are thesis-informed reconstructions, not
exact recovery of Emmanuel's Figure 4.3 settings. The three solvers retain
their native numerical settings and recombination treatments.

**Interpretation:** this supports the preliminary cross-code validation stage
before emulator work. CLASS is an independent comparator, not guaranteed truth.
Repeated runs matching exactly show repeatability, not numerical convergence.
Sampled maxima are not bounds between every possible k value.

We have not yet reproduced or trained the emulator, validated its full domain,
demonstrated parameter-inference effects, or established a fair three-solver
speed comparison.
